## Rulings (asked of the user 2026-10-14)
- Honorifics: kept, hyphenated (Pritz-san). Name order: Japanese.
- Romanisation source: official romaji where the game/manual/box has it, else Hepburn (no macrons).
- Credits: English.
- Abbreviations: full names and terms everywhere; prefer engine changes over shortening (flag any last-resort cut).
- Graphics text: all of it in scope (title logo is already English).
- Plans pre-approved: work through checkpoints, report at the end.

## Characters
No official romaji exists for this game's cast (checked 2026-10-14); names are the Western names the katakana spell.
Voices (from the char-select bios and banter lines 701B-7032):
- Pritz: ボク, playful and teasing, "multiple personalities, gender unknown" - keep gender-neutral (no he/she).
- Tanguy: オイラ, young dragon boy, brash and boastful ("I'm the strongest, y'know!").
- Ulrik: ワシ/のじゃ, old-woman channeler and cult leader (リク教), archaic grandma speech ("I daresay", "hmph").
- Cynthia: 私, chronically unlucky woman, gloomy and sighing.
- Dechtel: オレ様, vampire, swaggering braggart, "Gahaha!".
- Diana: 私/ですぅ, spirit of good luck, airy, sweet, overly polite ("Oh dear~").
